Privacy Policy for 12 Testers Community Hub

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Welcome to 12 Testers Community Hub ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the 12 Testers Community Hub mobile application and related services.

Important Notice: 12 Testers Community Hub is an independent platform designed to help Android developers connect and exchange peer testing for Google Play Console closed testing tracks. 12 Testers Community Hub is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC or Google Play Console.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide and improve our peer testing services:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture URL to create and authenticate your user account.
  • Application Listing Information: When you register an app for testing, we collect the details you provide, including your app name, package name, testing URL, app category, and description.
  • Activity & Credits Data: We track your in-app credit balance ("Moons"), app opt-in history, testing tasks completed, and bug feedback submitted to fellow developers.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Basic technical diagnostics (such as device model, OS version, and crash logs) collected automatically through Firebase services to maintain app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and authenticate user accounts.
  • To enable developers to list Android apps and discover community apps for testing.
  • To calculate and update credit balances ("Moons") based on your testing participation.
  • To facilitate community feedback and bug reports between developers.
  • To prevent fraud, spam, and platform abuse.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes trusted third-party service providers for authentication, database storage, and app stability. These third parties handle data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: Used for secure user sign-in.
  • Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Used for account management, secure database storage, and analytics.

4. Data Sharing & Visibility

  • Public App Listings: App details (title, package name, testing URL, and category) submitted by developers are visible to other logged-in members of the community so they can join your testing track.
  • Bug Reports & Feedback: Feedback submitted by testers is shared directly with the developer of the tested application.
  • No Selling of Data: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

5. No Guaranteed Approvals Disclaimer

12 Testers Community Hub provides a platform for real developers to connect and test each other's applications to help meet closed testing guidelines. However, production track approval and app publishing on the Google Play Store remain strictly at the discretion of Google Play Console policy review. We make no guaranteed claims regarding Google Play Console review outcomes.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. You may request full account and data deletion at any time by contacting us at the support email listed below. Upon receiving your request, your personal information and listed apps will be removed from our active database.

7. Children's Privacy

12 Testers Community Hub is intended solely for software developers and testers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
