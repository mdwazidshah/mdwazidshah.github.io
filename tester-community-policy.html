Tester Community Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Welcome to Tester Community ("we", "our", or "us"). This policy explains the acceptable use, content expectations, and community guidelines for developers and testers using Tester Community to share access to Google Play Console closed testing tracks.

Important Notice: Tester Community is an independent platform to help Android developers connect and exchange peer testing for Google Play Console closed testing tracks. Tester Community is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC or Google Play Console.

1. Purpose

Tester Community exists to help developers discover peers for closed testing and to provide constructive testing feedback. Use the platform only for legitimate testing collaboration and respectful interactions.

2. Acceptable Use

  • Only list apps that you own or have permission to distribute for testing.
  • Provide accurate app metadata (title, package name, testing URL, and category).
  • Use the platform to exchange testing participation fairly; do not manipulate credit balances or game the system.
  • Respect other members and keep communications professional and focused on testing.

3. Prohibited Activities

  • Do not submit malicious, infringing, or unlawful applications for testing.
  • Do not attempt to harvest personal data from other users or share private tester credentials.
  • Do not impersonate other developers or misrepresent app ownership.
  • Do not attempt to bypass Google Play Console policies or promote policy-violating content.

4. Reporting and Enforcement

If you encounter policy violations, spam, or abusive behavior, report the user or listing to our support team. Verified violations may result in removal of app listings, suspension of accounts, or permanent bans.

5. Relationship to Google Play Policies

Participation in Tester Community does not guarantee compliance with Google Play Console policies. Responsibility for ensuring your app meets Google Play policies and review requirements remains with you, the developer.

6. Data Handling

We process the minimal data required to operate the platform (account info, app listings, activity logs). For full details on data collection, use, and deletion, please see our privacy page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the platform or legal requirements. Users will be notified via the site and the updated policy will show a new "Last Updated" date.
