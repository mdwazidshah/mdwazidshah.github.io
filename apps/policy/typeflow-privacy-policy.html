Privacy Policy

Thank you for using AI Voice Keyboard – TypeFlow. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide, secure, and improve the TypeFlow experience.

  • Account and Contact Information: Name, phone number, and optional email when you register or contact support.
  • Identifiers: User ID, device ID, and advertising ID where required by the platform.
  • User Content: Typed text, voice input recordings, AI transcription data, and optional profile details that you voluntarily provide.
  • Usage Data: Keyboard activity, input preferences, language settings, and feature analytics.
  • Diagnostics: Crash reports, performance logs, and app stability data.

1.1 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not access or collect precise GPS location data, payment card details, or your device passwords. We do not use your personal spoken or typed content for targeted advertising without your clear consent.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To personalize keyboard suggestions and AI-assisted typing.
  • To provide voice-to-text, transcription, and correction features.
  • To maintain account access, support requests, and service reliability.
  • To analyze product performance and improve the app experience.
  • To send required notifications, reminders, or support messages.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

3. AI & Voice Processing

When you use voice input or AI-assisted text features, your input may be securely transmitted to our servers for transcription, analysis, and response generation. We use this data to provide immediate feedback and improve typing quality.

  • Voice recordings are retained only for a limited period for service quality and troubleshooting.
  • Transcripts and related analytics may be retained for product improvement, anonymized where possible.
  • We do not sell your personal voice content or messages to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

TypeFlow may use trusted service providers for analytics, crash reporting, push notifications, and attribution, subject to contractual safeguards. Examples include:

  • Firebase or similar analytics/crash tools for app stability and usage analysis.
  • Push notification services to deliver important app updates.
  • Ad attribution tools only when the user has granted tracking permission in the device settings.

We do not sell your personal data. Partner services process data under strict contractual terms and privacy safeguards.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services and meet legal obligations.

  • Account and contact information: Until account deletion or as legally required.
  • Voice/audio data: Up to 6 months unless a longer period is required for legal or security reasons.
  • Transcripts and usage logs: Up to 24 months, then anonymized or aggregated where appropriate.
  • Advertising IDs and attribution data: Up to 18 months or until reset.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time request:

  • Access to your personal data.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of your account and related data.
  • Withdrawal of marketing or tracking consent.

To exercise these rights, use the in-app Privacy screen or contact us via the support details below. We aim to respond within 30 days.

7. Children’s Privacy

If a child under the relevant age threshold uses the app through a parent or guardian account, we collect only the minimum information necessary for the service and do not use it for targeted advertising. Parents or guardians may request review or deletion of the child’s data by contacting us.

8. Security Measures

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including TLS encryption, secure storage practices, role-based access controls, and ongoing monitoring for security threats. No system is completely risk-free, but we continually improve our safeguards.

9. International Transfers

We may process data in locations outside your country, including India and other regions where our service providers operate. Where required, we use approved transfer mechanisms to protect your data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, notify users through the app or website. Continued use of the app after such changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
