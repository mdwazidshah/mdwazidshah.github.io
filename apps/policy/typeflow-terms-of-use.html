Privacy Policy

Welcome to TypeFlow (referred to as "we", "our", "us", or "the App", package: com.tools.typeflow.ai). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a transparent understanding of how your data is handled.

TypeFlow is a voice dictation and English refinement utility designed to transcribe your spoken voice in Indian vernacular languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, and Indian English) and convert it into polished, ready-to-send English text.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our policies and practices regarding your information and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Voice and Audio Data (RECORD_AUDIO)

  • What We Access: When you explicitly tap the microphone button or the floating assistant orb, TypeFlow accesses your device's microphone to capture your spoken words.
  • How It Is Used: Audio data is converted to text either on-device via Android's built-in SpeechRecognizer service or securely processed in real-time to generate transcriptions.
  • Data Retention: Audio data is processed ephemerally. We do NOT record, store, sell, or archive your raw voice recordings on any remote server. Once the spoken words are transcribed into text, the audio session is immediately terminated and discarded.

B. Dictated Text and Polished Output

  • What We Access: The transcribed text you dictate or type into the App.
  • How It Is Used: The text is analyzed exclusively to correct grammar, adapt tone (Professional, Casual Chat, Short & Direct, Grammar Polish), and translate vernacular phrases into fluent English.
  • Data Retention: Your transcriptions and polished messages are stored locally on your device in your app's local storage (e.g., Saved tab). You can edit, bookmark, or delete these items at any time. We do not store your private message content on external servers.

C. Authentication Information (Optional Google Sign-In)

  • What We Access: If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information provided by Google OAuth (your name, email address, and profile photo URL).
  • How It Is Used: This information is used solely to authenticate your account, personalize your in-app experience, and manage subscription/feature access.
  • Guest Mode: You can use TypeFlow without an account using the "Continue as Guest" option. No personal profile data is collected in Guest Mode.

D. Device and Diagnostic Information

  • What We Access: Non-personally identifiable technical information, such as device model, OS version, app version, language preference, and crash logs.
  • How It Is Used: To identify bugs, prevent crashes, and ensure compatibility across Android devices.

2. Accessibility Service API Prominent Disclosure

TypeFlow uses the Android Accessibility Service API (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) exclusively to provide its core universal voice dictation and direct-paste feature across third-party apps.

Exact Purpose of the Accessibility Service:

  1. Text Field Detection (TYPE_VIEW_FOCUSED, TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED): To identify when you tap into an editable text field in other apps (such as messaging, email, or browser apps) so the floating Flow assistant bubble can appear conveniently near your cursor.
  2. Direct Text Insertion (ACTION_SET_TEXT, ACTION_PASTE): To paste your dictated and polished English text directly into the active text field without requiring you to switch keyboards or manually copy-paste.
  3. Keyboard Visibility Detection (TYPE_WINDOWS_CHANGED): To show or dismiss the floating assistant bubble appropriately when your on-screen keyboard opens or closes.

Privacy Guarantees Regarding the Accessibility Service:

  • No Personal Data Collection: TypeFlow does NOT read, collect, monitor, store, or transmit any text you type or receive in other apps.
  • No Password or Credential Monitoring: We never interact with password fields, credit card inputs, or sensitive banking screens.
  • No Audio Surveillance: The Accessibility Service does NOT record audio or monitor background activity.
  • No Deceptive Practices: TypeFlow does not bypass Android's built-in security controls, alter system settings, or prevent users from disabling the service at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility > Downloaded Apps > TypeFlow.

3. Overlay Permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

TypeFlow requests the "Display over other apps" (Overlay) permission solely to render the floating Flow Assistant bubble on your screen while using other applications. You can enable or disable this floating overlay at any time within the App's Settings.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • All network communications (such as authentication or AI processing) occur over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.
  • Local app data is protected by Android's application sandboxing.
  • We do not maintain unencrypted remote databases of your personal dictations.

6. User Control and Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data within TypeFlow:

  • Clear Local History: You can delete all saved flows and recent transcriptions directly from the Saved tab.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke Microphone, Accessibility, or Overlay permissions at any time via Android's System Settings.
  • Account Deletion: If you signed in with Google and wish to delete your account and associated profile data, you can do so in the App Settings or by contacting us at hazroonkhatoon.dev@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

TypeFlow is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
