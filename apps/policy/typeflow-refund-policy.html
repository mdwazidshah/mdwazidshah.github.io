Refund Policy

At TypeFlow, we aim to provide a clear and fair refund process for our premium services and in-app purchases. This Refund Policy explains when a refund may be granted and how to request one.

1. General Policy

Most digital purchases and subscriptions are non-refundable once access has been granted unless a valid exception applies. However, we understand that sometimes a purchase may not meet your expectations or may have been made accidentally.

2. Eligibility for Refund

Refund requests may be considered in the following situations:

  • Payment was made in error or duplicate purchase was charged.
  • The app failed to deliver the promised functionality at the time of purchase.
  • There was a technical issue preventing access to the purchased features.
  • The purchase was made without a clear understanding of the service and is requested promptly.

We reserve the right to refuse refunds where the service has already been substantially used, the subscription has been active for a long period, or the request is made after the applicable refund window.

3. Subscription Cancellations

If you are on a recurring subscription, you may cancel future renewals at any time through the app store or account settings. Cancellation prevents future renewals, but it does not guarantee a refund for the current billing period unless the policy or platform terms allow it.

4. Request Process

To request a refund, please contact us through the app support screen or email us at hello@getsupernova.ai with:

  • App name and purchase details.
  • Order or transaction ID.
  • Date of purchase.
  • Reason for the refund request.

5. Review Timeline

We review refund requests within a reasonable time and will notify you of the outcome by email or through the app. Where approved, refunds are processed through the original payment method according to the applicable payment provider’s timeline.

6. Refund Exceptions

We may deny refunds in cases of:

  • Material misuse of the service.
  • Repeated requests after excessive use.
  • Violations of the App’s Terms and Conditions.
  • Requests made after the applicable refund deadline.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted in the app or on our website. Continued use of the paid service after the effective date indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For all refund-related questions, write to us at hello@getsupernova.ai or contact our support team through the app.
