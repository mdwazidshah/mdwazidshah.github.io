Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. It is Md Wazid Ali's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect while operating our app, Volume Lock Pro.

1. Information We Collect

Volume Lock Pro is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage information to external servers.

2. Device Permissions

The app requires access to system volume settings and "Do Not Disturb" permissions solely to perform its core function: locking and controlling your device's volume levels. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use Google Play Services for core functionality and updates. These services may collect information as per their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected by us, your data remains secure within your device's own security environment.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at hello@wazidali.dev.
